AI Video Editor

I edit footage shot on camera and footage made with AI — and increasingly both in the same timeline. Cut in Premiere Pro, graded as one film, with generated shots and effects added where the footage needs them.

Editing with AI, not by AI

AI doesn't decide the cut. Rhythm, performance, what stays and what goes — that is still editing. What AI changes is what the editor can do with the material: generate the insert you didn't get on the day, add a VFX shot that would have needed a 3D team, extend a shot, or build an entire sequence that was never filmed.

So the edit is no longer limited to what was shot. That is the part of the job I specialise in: making generated material and real footage cut together so nobody can tell where one ends.

What I edit

  • Music videos — live-action sessions and full productions, cut to the track.
  • Commercials and brand spots — from a single hero film to a set of cut-downs.
  • Vertical content — 9:16 edits for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
  • AI-generated shots and VFX — added to your footage, matched in light, grain and colour.
  • Colour grading — one consistent look across filmed and generated shots.

How it works

  1. Material

    You send the footage, the music and any references. If there is no footage yet, the shots can be generated.

  2. First cut

    A full cut of the piece, so the structure is agreed before the detail work.

  3. AI shots and VFX

    Missing inserts, effects and extra shots are generated to match the footage around them.

  4. Sound and grade

    Sound pass and one colour grade across everything.

  5. Versions

    Final master plus the cut-downs and aspect ratios each platform needs.

Questions

Can you add AI shots to footage I've already filmed?

Yes — that is exactly what was done in Habar N-ai: a live-action music video with AI-generated VFX shots cut in. The generated shots are matched to the footage in light, colour and grain.

What do you need from me?

The footage (or the brief, if it is all to be generated), the music or voice-over, the deadline, and two or three references for the feel you want.

Do you edit vertical videos?

Yes. Every project can be delivered in 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts alongside the 16:9 master.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in Barcelona and edit for clients in Spain, Romania and elsewhere; footage is exchanged online.
