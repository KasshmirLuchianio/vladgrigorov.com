About

I'm Vlad Grigorov, a filmmaker working entirely inside generative tools. I treat AI models the way a director treats a crew — casting light, framing and motion until the frame feels alive.

My work sits between cinema and code: the discipline of storytelling, the speed of a new medium. No sets to rent, no gear to truck — just a vision pushed through the machine until it holds.
