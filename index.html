About

I'm Vlad Grigorov, a film director and cinematographer based in Bucharest, working across live-action production and generative tools. I shoot and edit real-world footage, then bring the same eye for light, framing, rhythm and performance to AI-directed work.

My work sits between cinema and code: hands-on cinematography and post-production on one side, generative storytelling and rapid creative workflows on the other. Whether the image comes through a camera or a model, the goal is the same — make the frame feel alive.
