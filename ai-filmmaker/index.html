AI Filmmaker

I write, direct and edit films made with generative video models — music videos, commercials and short films. I shoot live action too, so the AI work is held to the same standard as footage that came off a camera.

What an AI filmmaker actually does

The model renders the frames. Everything that makes them a film is still a decision someone has to make: who is on screen and how they stay the same person from shot to shot, the look, the lens, where the camera sits and how it moves, what each shot is for, and where it cuts. That is the job.

Without it you get clips — impressive for three seconds, unusable together. With it you get a sequence that holds up as one piece of work, with a consistent character, a consistent world and an edit that carries the story.

How a project runs

  1. Brief

    What it is for, where it will be seen, how long it runs, and the references you already like.

  2. Treatment and look

    The idea, the visual direction and the shot list, agreed before anything is generated.

  3. Character and world

    Recurring people are built as trained identity models so the same face holds across every shot; locations and wardrobe are fixed the same way.

  4. Shots

    Each shot is directed and generated on its own — framing, camera move, light — and redone until it is usable, not until it is merely interesting.

  5. Edit, sound and grade

    Cut in Premiere Pro, then sound and a single colour grade across everything, so generated and filmed shots sit together.

  6. Delivery

    Masters in the formats you need — 16:9, and 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

Tools

Kling, Seedance and Higgsfield for video; trained character models for consistent faces; Nano Banana and image models for stills and start frames; Premiere Pro for the edit. The tools change every few months. The decisions above do not, which is why the work is organised around them and not around any one model.

Questions

Can AI video keep the same person across shots?

Yes, if the person is built properly first. I train an identity model from reference photos and generate every shot from it. It is not automatic — some shots drift and get regenerated — but the finished film shows one consistent person.

Do you only work with AI?

No. I also shoot and edit live-action music videos, and I mix the two: real footage with generated shots inside it. The full range is on the portfolio.

Do you work outside Barcelona?

Yes. I'm based in Barcelona and work with clients in Spain, Romania and elsewhere. Most AI projects run remotely from brief to delivery.

How much does an AI film cost?

It depends on the length, the number of shots and whether a character or world has to be built first. Send the brief and you get a fixed quote before anything starts.
